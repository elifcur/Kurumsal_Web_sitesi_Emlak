# Business

## Business template deliberately departs from the shared serif look
templates/business follows the customer's Nova Market supermarket mockup: Poppins (sans) display face, green primary with orange CTAs, two-row header with a product search, round category icons and promo bands. Do not "fix" it back to the serif hero used by the other templates. Its demo assets are cut from the mockup and live in public/images/templates/business; category icons are PNGs used both as category and product images, so product cards render them with object-contain on a cream panel.
